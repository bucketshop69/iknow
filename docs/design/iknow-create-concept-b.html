This market resolves on whether Apple publicly announces an iPhone model with a foldable display on or before September 30, 2026.

Rules

  • The product must be an iPhone, not only an iPad, patent, or rumor.

The market can be invalid if Apple removes public evidence, the date is ambiguous, or the announcement cannot be verified from official materials.
